The Story Behind Vexara AI

I'm Shivam Sah, a 16-year-old student developer and founder from Kathmandu, Nepal. I created Vexara AI because I saw how many students like me struggle with maths, science, and other subjects when good help isn't available or is too expensive.

The Problem: During my Class 9 and 10, I faced the same challenges as thousands of SEE students. Math classes moved too fast, finding good tutors was expensive, and most AI tools weren't built for Nepal's SEE curriculum.

The Solution: I decided to build Vexara AI — an AI study assistant built specifically for SEE students. I coded the backend in Python Flask, integrated Google Gemini API with Groq optimization, built the frontend from scratch, and trained the system with the complete NEB mathematics curriculum.

Why Free: Education is a human right. Vexara AI is completely free because quality learning support should never be behind a paywall. Every student in Nepal deserves access to powerful AI learning tools.

My Vision: I want Vexara to become Nepal's go-to AI learning platform. But this is just the beginning — I'm building towards a larger mission: making world-class education accessible to every student, regardless of where they live or how much money their family has.
